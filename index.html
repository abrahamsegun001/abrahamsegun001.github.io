01 · About

The question behind my work.

Mathematics taught me to describe complex systems through models. Working with neural recordings raised a harder question: if a model reproduces brain activity, what have we actually learned about the processes that generated it? Good predictions and reliable explanations are not always the same thing.

At PAUSTI, with guidance from Prof. Miguel Maravall at the University of Sussex, I investigate cortical activity recorded with widefield calcium imaging. By comparing neural-field models and testing parameter inference, I examine which interpretations the data can support and which remain uncertain.

Working on spectroscopy methods and the generalization of medical imaging models has reinforced the same principle: conclusions depend on measurements, assumptions, and validation. I bring that perspective to my study of neural systems.

Ongoing · MSc research

When does a model explain cortical activity?

Different model parameters can account for similar recordings. My MSc work explores how reliably neural-field parameters can be inferred from widefield calcium imaging, using model comparison and controlled simulations to test identifiability.

Research in progress. Preliminary results are not presented as established findings.

MICCAI MIRASOL · 2026

Will glioma segmentation models generalize across MRI datasets?

Led methodology and analysis in a collaborative benchmark of three segmentation architectures trained on BraTS 2021 and evaluated on BraTS-Africa. The published results show why performance on one dataset cannot be assumed to transfer to another.
